Process

How Our Referral Partner Fulfillment Works

A straightforward, relationship-first process from onboarding to qualified referral relationships.

01
Step 01
Tell Us About Your Business
Before fulfillment begins, we collect the information needed to understand your ideal referral market — so every outreach effort is relevant to your services and service area.
Service Area ZIP Codes Insurance Accepted Client Types Specialties Services Offered Ideal Referral Source
The more accurately we understand your business and ideal client, the more relevant the professionals we target can be. We don't begin outreach until we have a clear picture of your services, location, population served, and what an ideal referral relationship looks like for you.
02
Step 02
We Find Relevant Professionals
Our fulfillment team researches your target area and identifies professionals who may be relevant referral sources for your specific services.
Case Managers Discharge Planners Social Workers Care Coordinators Other Relevant Professionals
The exact professionals we target depend on your service type, geographic market, and ideal client profile. We focus on finding individuals who are positioned to encounter clients that may benefit from your services — not a generic list.
03
Step 03
We Reach Out
We contact relevant professionals on your behalf and introduce your services in a professional, relationship-focused manner.
Professional
"Do you work with clients who may need home health or specialized care services?"
Our Team
"We work with a provider serving your area and wanted to see whether their services may be relevant to clients you support. Would you be open to learning more?"
Our outreach is professional and direct — not mass-email or aggressive cold calling. We open a genuine conversation about whether your services may be relevant to the clients these professionals support. We represent your practice professionally at every touchpoint.
04
Step 04
Interested Professionals Move Forward
When a professional shows genuine interest and appears relevant to your services, we advance the opportunity.
Potential Professional
→
Interested
→
Relevant
→
Ready to Connect
We do not make the final relationship decision for you. That belongs to you and your team.
05
Step 05
Meeting With You
Interested professionals are connected with your team so you can speak with them directly.
Our Team
→
Your Team
→
Referral Professional
You will discuss your services, their needs, the clients they serve, their organization, and whether there is a genuine opportunity to work together. This is your conversation — we facilitate the connection, you build the relationship.
06
Step 06
You Decide If It's a Fit
The final decision belongs entirely to you. If you approve the professional and both sides agree to build a relationship, they become a qualified referral partner.
Good Fit
Becomes a Qualified Referral Partner — counts toward your plan
Not a Fit
Rejected contact is not counted toward your plan
If you reject a professional we introduce, we do not count that contact toward your qualified-partner plan. You only count professionals you approve.
07
Step 07
Build the Relationship
Once you and the professional agree to work together, the relationship is yours to maintain — for as long as you choose.
No recurring referral fee Long-term relationship Your connection to keep
There is no recurring referral fee paid to the partner. AccelRCM provides the relationship-building and fulfillment service — the actual professional relationship belongs to you. You can maintain, develop, and grow that relationship on your own terms going forward.
